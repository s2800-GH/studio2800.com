## Suggested Design Settings In GoDaddy

- Style: bold technology / technical poster / AI video production studio
- Primary color: dark graphite or near-black
- Accent colors: orange and teal
- Secondary accents: muted green and amber
- Fonts: use a heavy condensed uppercase heading font if available; pair with a clean sans-serif body font
- Buttons: orange primary button with dark text, outlined secondary button
- Layout: full-width hero, three-column outcome strip, two-image showcase gallery, four video-lane cards, three-step process, contact form near the bottom
